# Provenance and licences

English | [中文](provenance.zh-CN.md)

> The original folder also held 7 older LUTs. The GRKit licence notes below cover only the 13 GRKit-derived files; they grant no open redistribution or commercial rights for the older ones, whose original copyright notices travelled with the cubes unchanged.

11 spektrafilm-derived LUTs are CC BY-SA 4.0. Author: Andrea Volpato, <https://github.com/andreavolpato/spektrafilm>. The pinned commit and the per-file mapping are in `manifest.json` and `SOURCES.json`. This pack adapted the input to Standard and resampled; changes are listed in `CHANGELOG.txt`, and the full notice is in `licenses/SPEKTRAFILM_LICENSE.txt`. A shared modified version must keep the same licence and the attribution.

2 Spectral Film LUT derivatives are MIT. Author: Jan Lohse, <https://github.com/JanLohse/spectral_film_lut>. Full notice in `licenses/SPECTRAL_FILM_LUT_LICENSE.txt`.

The recipe work and the fitting project come from GRKit: <https://github.com/ahonn/grkit>, with notices in `licenses/GRKit-NOTICE.md` and `licenses/GRKit-MIT-LICENSE.txt`. The compatible LUTs were rebuilt from the source recipes; they do not copy GRKit's published fitted parameters. Packing files with different licences together does not change any of them.

Film and brand names describe the emulated stock. No brand has endorsed this pack, and none of it has been verified against real film. Original copyright and licence notices stay as they are.
